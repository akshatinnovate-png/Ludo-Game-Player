Ludo Player

Point your phone at the board. It reads the position and tells you the best move for its two colours.

  1. Pick the board's colours and which two colours are the computer's team.
  2. Take one photo with every token in its yard so the app learns your board.
  3. Each turn: enter the throws, take a photo, and confirm what happened.
  4. On the computer's turns, the best move is drawn on the photo. You move the tokens for it.
